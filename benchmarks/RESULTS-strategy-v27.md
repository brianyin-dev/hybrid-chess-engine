# v27 independent heuristic confirmation — stopped by user

The frozen v26 strategic heuristic was compared with the original heuristic, with unchanged search and LMR. No NN was trained or used. Planned 200 games at 250ms followed by 40 games at the app budget (750ms, depth cap eight), on fresh paired book starts.

Stopped at the user's request: 132 completed games, 66 wins, 16 draws, 50 losses; 56.06% score. One in-progress game was interrupted and is excluded from completed-game score. This is a stopped partial experiment, not a completed confirmation. The app-budget game test did not run. No evaluator was promoted.

Known-failure screen: losing transitions 9→6 at 250ms and 8→5 at 750ms; >=150cp errors 9→8 and 8→7. These previously studied cases are development evidence, not independent strength evidence. Before games, all 126 tests passed.

The predeclared candidate, openings, protocol, per-move records and partial PGNs are preserved. Do not combine this interrupted sample with the earlier v26 games as if it were one predeclared confirmation.
